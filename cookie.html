Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains how ScholarRoads, a brand of HME Technology Corp, uses cookies and similar technologies.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website can store on your device. They're often used to remember preferences or to track how a site is used.

The cookies we use

Analytics & advertising cookies — we do not use these at all. ScholarRoads sets no analytics, tracking, or advertising cookies, and we do not collect data about your visit. The site is purely informational and simply links out to lenders.

Third-party sites

When you click a link to a lender such as College Ave or Sallie Mae, you leave our site and that company may set its own cookies under its own policy. We don't control those.

Global Privacy Control (GPC)

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, there's nothing for us to opt you out of, since we don't run tracking in the first place.

Changes

If we ever introduce cookies (for example analytics in the future), they would only be set with your consent where required, and we'd update this policy first.

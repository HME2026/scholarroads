Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information ScholarRoads ("we", "us"), a brand of HME Technology Corp, handles when you visit our website. We keep it deliberately simple.

What we collect

Nothing personal. We do not ask you to create an account or submit any personal or financial details to use this site, and we do not collect personal information about you. We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising tools, we do not use cookies to track you, and we do not sell any data. Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability, as every website does — we do not use these to identify you.

Links to lenders

When you click a link to a lender such as College Ave or Sallie Mae, you leave ScholarRoads and that company collects and processes your information under its own privacy policy. Please review the privacy policy of any site you visit or apply with.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we don't collect personal information, there's nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have privacy rights regarding data held by the lenders and services you use — direct those requests to those companies. We honor recognized browser opt-out signals, including Global Privacy Control (GPC), where applicable.

Changes

If we ever add analytics or other data tools, we will update this policy first and, where required, ask for your consent. When we make changes, we'll revise the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions? Use our Contact page.
